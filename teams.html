The people behind EthiCA

Expertise across ethics, law and biomedical innovation.

Meet the specialists supporting responsible research, privacy and emerging technologies.

Director

Dr. Başak Kale

Oxon (DPhil.), LSE (MSc.)

Dr. Başak Kale brings extensive experience as an independent ethics expert for the European Commission, covering project development, management, monitoring, auditing and assessment in research, business ethics and the social sciences.

Her teaching and research appointments have included UBC, Simon Fraser University, Harvard, UC Berkeley, Hitotsubashi University, Boğaziçi University and METU. She has consulted for UNHCR, the Council of Europe, OSCE and the UN.

Qualifications: DPhil (University of Oxford), PhD (METU), MSc (LSE), BSc (METU).

Konstantinos Gkatzis, Ph.D.

Dr. Konstantinos Gkatzis is a biomedical scientist, biotechnology entrepreneur, and innovation expert with over 20 years of experience in drug discovery, preclinical development, cardiovascular research, stem cell biology, and emerging biomedical technologies.

His scientific and technical expertise encompasses human pluripotent stem cells, human embryonic tissues, organoids, organ-on-chip systems, human cell-based disease models, phenotypic screening, and AI-enabled drug discovery, with particular emphasis on translational research and the development of innovative therapeutic and screening platforms.

He is currently CTO and Co-Founder of VICAP AI (vicapai.io) and previously served as COO and Co-Founder of KSILINK/Myrtil Biotechnologies, where he led drug discovery and biotechnology development programmes. He also served as Investment Principal at Solyco Capital UK and Europe and has extensive experience in technology commercialisation, intellectual property-driven innovation, and translating scientific discoveries into commercially viable applications.

As a European Commission scientific expert, he has evaluated over 400 research and innovation proposals representing approximately €1.5 billion in funding requests, developing substantial experience in scientific assessment, innovation evaluation, research project risk analysis as well as scientific ethical compliance.

His interests in ethics advisory and technical consultancy focus on the responsible development and application of advanced biomedical technologies, particularly where scientific innovation intersects with ethical considerations, human biological materials, AI, data privacy and clinical translation.

Konstantinos’ Specific Areas of Interest for Ethics Advisory and Technical Consultancy

  • Human stem cell and embryonic tissue research: Ethical considerations surrounding human pluripotent stem cells, embryonic tissue, human biological materials, consent, provenance, and responsible research practices.
  • Organoids and organ-on-chip technologies: Ethical and scientific oversight of human cell-derived models, advanced in vitro systems, and emerging alternatives to animal experimentation.
  • Drug discovery and preclinical development: Scientific and ethical evaluation of therapeutic discovery programmes, phenotypic screening, preclinical models, and translational research.
  • Cardiovascular research: Technical assessment and ethical considerations in cardiovascular disease modelling, human cell-based platforms, and therapeutic development.
  • AI-driven drug discovery: Responsible AI, algorithmic transparency, data governance, bias, and validation of AI-enabled biomedical research platforms.
  • Research commercialisation and translation: Ethical considerations in intellectual property, exploitation of research results, industry–academic collaboration, technology transfer, and commercial use of human-derived research materials.
  • Independent scientific and technical evaluation: Expert review of Horizon Europe and UK-funded projects, including scientific feasibility, technological risk, research integrity, and responsible innovation.

Giannis Kyriakidis, Ph.D.

Dr. Kyriakidis is an accomplished lawyer with over 28 years career in legal advisory and representation specializing in data protection law, IT Law, Immigration Law and Public Law.  He has a proven track record in implementing GDPR compliance strategies. He has ten years of experience as an independent Ethics Expert for the European Commission.

He has ethically evaluated and monitored more than 400 research and innovative projects. He is also a Certified Adult Educator with extensive teaching experience (Greek National School of Public Administration and Local Government (ESDDA).

He combines legal expertise, institutional knowledge and high professional integrity with educational leadership to train professionals on complex regulatory and ethical frameworks, including public governance, data protection, AI law and human rights. His current work focuses on the ethical and responsible development, deployment, and use of artificial intelligence, AI governance, and the evolving legal and regulatory landscape surrounding emerging technologies. He is particularly committed to promoting transparency, accountability, fairness, and human rights in AI-driven decision-making, while supporting organisations in developing robust ethical compliance frameworks and responsible AI practices.

Charles Meyer, BCL (Oxon), MLitt. (Oxon), JD, BSc., MSc.

Charles Meyer is an accomplished international technology lawyer, AI law specialist, trainer, and legal educator with over 30 years of experience in intellectual property, data protection, privacy, corporate law, and regulatory compliance. His professional focus increasingly centres on artificial intelligence, particularly the intersection of AI law, ethics, governance, and the responsible integration of emerging technologies into legal and business practices.

As a Law Expert in AI research at Mercor Intelligence, Charles works directly with artificial intelligence systems and large language models (LLMs), contributing his legal expertise to AI-related research and development. In his role as an instructor at AltaClaro, he educates legal professionals on prompt engineering, AI literacy, and the effective use of generative AI in legal practice, helping bridge the gap between technological advancement and professional legal expertise.

With advanced legal qualifications from the University of Oxford and American University, alongside professional credentials in patent law, privacy, and arbitration, Charles brings a multidisciplinary perspective to the evolving AI landscape. He is passionate about exploring the legal and ethical implications of artificial intelligence, promoting responsible AI adoption, and empowering legal professionals to navigate the opportunities and challenges of AI-driven transformation while maintaining high standards of accountability, professional integrity, and regulatory compliance.

Charles brings extensive legal expertise and professional experience across both North America (the USA and Canada) and the UK, offering a valuable transatlantic perspective on legal, regulatory, and technological developments, particularly in the evolving fields of AI law, data protection, and regulatory compliance.
